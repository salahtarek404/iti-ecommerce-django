About Amazon

Learn more about our mission, history, and values.

Our Mission

Amazon’s mission is to be Earth’s most customer-centric company, where customers can find and discover anything they might want to buy online, and endeavors to offer its customers the lowest possible prices.

Our History

Founded in 1994 by Jeff Bezos, Amazon started as an online bookstore and has grown into a global marketplace, offering millions of products across various categories, from electronics to groceries, with a focus on innovation and customer satisfaction.

Our Values

Amazon is guided by four principles: customer obsession, passion for invention, commitment to operational excellence, and long-term thinking. We strive to innovate and improve every day to serve our customers better.
